# Historical-Case Retrieval Example

## FOMC_0002

The current event is compared with FOMC_0013 (18 June 2025), FOMC_0027 (31 July 2024), and FOMC_0011 (30 July 2025). All three combine an unchanged policy rate with language about inflation, labor conditions, and uncertainty, but their shock composition and market reactions differ.

- **FOMC_0013:** highest overall statement similarity (0.9257) and lowest macro-state distance (0.1400). Historical returns were SPY −0.02%, QQQ −0.02%, GLD −0.54%, and UUP 0.18%.
- **FOMC_0027:** overall similarity 0.9225 and macro-state distance 0.3495. More clearly dovish inflation and labor wording coincided with SPY 1.63%, QQQ 2.96%, GLD 1.81%, and UUP −0.55%.
- **FOMC_0011:** strongest shock-vector similarity (0.9245) with overall similarity 0.9211. Historical returns were SPY −0.13%, QQQ 0.13%, GLD −1.73%, and UUP 0.98%.

These cases provide mixed signals, especially for QQQ and GLD. The retrieval output should therefore be treated as conditional historical evidence rather than a deterministic analogue.
